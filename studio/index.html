Album styles 
      

  
A singer is chosen per section in the lead vocal timeline: their section moves to their range in the ABC, their lyrics tag says who sings and the song's singers are declared in the style (that is how YuE2 builds duets). An extra voice has a single singer. A band or genre is chosen as the song's base style. Songs keep the reference: editing a style here changes every song that uses it.

Freeze voice

  

    
Choose generated audio where this singer sings (takes or extra voices of the open song; listen with ▶). They are joined into a single audio file and a LoRA is trained with the song's lyrics and style; when it finishes it is assigned to the singer. In YuE2 the style text does not decide the voice: the LoRA does bring it closer.
